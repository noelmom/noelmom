### 🚧 Currently building

- **[RevLink Sidecar](https://revlinkgarage.com)** — an ESP32-P4 that gives a COBB AccessPort a laptop-free home for its data. Plug the AccessPort in, open a page on your phone, and your datalogs and maps are there. Built it because my girlfriend's WRX needed a laptop for every step between her and her tuner. Firmware, hardware and a printable case, all open source. *Free on GitHub, flashes from your browser.*
- **[EasyTunnel](https://noelmom.github.io/easytunnel-public/)** — an iPhone-first SSH client with local port forwarding you can actually *browse*, plus a terminal, SFTP, and a code editor. *On the App Store.*
- **[Vox](https://noelmom.github.io/vox/)** — a Mac studio for turning scripts into natural speech, rendered entirely on-device. *Shipped.*
